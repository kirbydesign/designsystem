Lists are made up of multiple rows of items which can contain text, buttons, toggles, icons, thumbnails, and much more. Lists generally contain items with similar data content, such as images and text.

Lists support several interactions including swiping items to reveal options, dragging to reorder items within the list, and deleting items.

Accessibility

If the list has no apparent heading or does not in itself communicate a clear purpose, consider adding an accessible name to help screen reader users understand the purpose and content of the list.

It is possible to provide an accessible name for your list using the aria-label or aria-labelledby attribute on the kirby-list element.

The label you provide will be announced together with the number of items in the list.

Importing
