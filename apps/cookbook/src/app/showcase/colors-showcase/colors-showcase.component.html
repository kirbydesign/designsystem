Color tokens describe the role a color plays, not the color itself. They are named --kirby-color-[property]-[role]-[emphasis]-[state] , where property is fill , content or border .

Every token is redefined on each surface: kirby-surface-base , kirby-surface-raised and kirby-surface-brand . A component using --kirby-color-fill-base always gets the color of the surface it sits on. The previews below show each token on all three surfaces.

Most roles also have -hover and -active variants for interaction states, e.g. --kirby-color-fill-raised-hover .
